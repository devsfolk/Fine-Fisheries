Our process

From raw material to bagged feed — quality at every step.

A five-stage pipeline ensures consistent pellet density, nutrient profile, and shelf life for every batch we ship.

01

Raw material intake

Material is chipped and staged in bulk before processing begins.

02

Rotary drying

Moisture is reduced in the rotary drum dryer for stable, mould-free feed.

03

Milling & mixing

Dried material is ground and blended into a uniform base mix.

04

Pelletizing

The mix is extruded, cooled, and sized into pellets or mash.

05

Packing & dispatch

Feed is bagged, checked, and staged for bulk or retail dispatch.
