About us

Built around one processing line, run with care.

Fine Fisheries is a Karachi-based fish feed manufacturer, producing aquafeed for farms and distributors from a single dedicated facility in Deh Rehri.

Our story

From raw material to a trusted feed brand.

Fine Fisheries was set up to give local fish farms a dependable, locally produced alternative to imported feed. Every batch runs through our own rotary drying, milling, and pelletizing line, so we control quality from the raw material stage through to the packed bag.

  • Rotary drum drying for stable, low-moisture feed
  • In-house milling, mixing and pelletizing
  • Four feed grades covering fry through to grow-out stock
  • Bulk and bagged dispatch for farms and distributors

How we work

Consistency starts with the raw material.

Material is chipped and staged before it ever reaches the dryer, so the moisture reduction and milling stages that follow behave the same way, batch after batch. That consistency is what carries through into pellet size, texture, and shelf life.
